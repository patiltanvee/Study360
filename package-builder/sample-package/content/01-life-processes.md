# Life processes

## Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make their own food using sunlight, carbon dioxide and water. It takes place in the chloroplasts, which contain the green pigment chlorophyll.

The overall equation is: carbon dioxide + water, in the presence of sunlight and chlorophyll, gives glucose + oxygen.

### Steps

Chlorophyll absorbs light energy. Water is split into hydrogen and oxygen. Carbon dioxide is reduced to carbohydrates. Oxygen is released through the stomata.

## Respiration

Respiration releases energy from food. Aerobic respiration uses oxygen and gives carbon dioxide, water and energy. Anaerobic respiration happens without oxygen; in yeast it gives ethanol and carbon dioxide, and in muscles it gives lactic acid.
